Definitions follow the Marketing Standards & Trigger System. Marketing lead = HubSpot deal with a lead source (“Known lead source”, else the original traffic source for paid search, organic search and social); rep-created deals with no source are self-sourced. Spend = working media from the QuickBooks advertising ledger, excluding software and the HBA amortisation; event true costs are charged by leads. Cost per lead uses leads created that month; qualified CPL removes leads killed at phone. Jobs = Closed Won deals with an Aspire Opportunity #; revenue = HubSpot deal amount. Return on spend = job revenue ÷ spend, credited to the lead month and judged only once the month is 90 days old. Service line = “Type of Job”.
